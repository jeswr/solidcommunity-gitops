This is a community server for developers and early adopters. Don't store personal, sensitive or commercial data in your Pod here. Read the acceptable use policy.

How it works

Solid separates your data from the apps that use it, so you can switch apps without losing anything.

  1. Create your Pod

    Sign up with your email address. You get a Pod for your data and a WebID, your identity on the Solid web.

  2. Log in to Solid apps

    Use your WebID to log in to any Solid app. Apps read and write data in your Pod instead of keeping it themselves.

  3. Decide who sees what

    Keep data private, share it with particular people and apps, or make it public. You can change your mind at any time.

New to Solid?

Solid is an open standard for personal data stores, started by Sir Tim Berners-Lee.

Learn about Solid What Solid is and why it matters, at solidproject.org. Find apps Apps that work with your Pod, from notes to social networks. Ask the community Questions, ideas and help on the Solid forum.

When you use Solid, information is stored in your Solid pod and the Solid pods of other users, depending on the actions you take and the apps you use. You and other Solid pod users control who has access to that information. Some information, like your Solid profile, is public.

As a user of solidcommunity.net, you have the responsibility to understand what data you are storing in your Pod, with whom that data is shared, and what, if any, data you have made public.
